## Suggested captions

- **E1:** Classical hitting time and quantum search time versus graph size for
  complete graphs, cycles, 2D grids, and hypercubes. Lines are power-law fits.
- **E2 success:** Success probability versus walk steps for single, independent,
  and clustered marked sets on grid and barbell graphs. Shaded bands show the
  min--max range over three random marked sets.
- **E2 HT:** Classical hitting time versus extended hitting time for the grid and
  barbell cases(left)and for the AGJK adversarial torus construction(right).
- **E3:** Toy semantic graph, and target activation from the cue `animal` to
  `dog` and `violin`. Time is normalized by the spectral gap.
- **E4:** Probability of the uncoupled option(s) versus coupling for the
  first-crossing, random-stopping, detection and classical readouts, with
  bands over laziness and edge-weight jitter; spread per readout.
- **E5:** Readout success heatmap over $(T, p)$, detection-model curves
  $F_p(T)$, best rate $p^\ast(T)$ versus deadline, and $F_p(40)$ versus $p$.
